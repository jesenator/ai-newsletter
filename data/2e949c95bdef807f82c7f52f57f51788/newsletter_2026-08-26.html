Top: Cyber Agent Escapes Its Sandbox

  • Trail of Bits: GPT-5.6-Cyber broke out of a QEMU/KVM VM three separate times Trail of Bits X: trailofbits Given only a "read the flag" CTF task, it used a recent kernel CVE, then a chained libslirp bug, and — after everything was rebuilt from upstream — found three fresh 0-days and chained them into a reliable escape. Extremely CivAI-shaped, and directly relevant given the recent containment breaches.

Transformer: The Surveillance State

  • "We're sleepwalking into an AI surveillance dystopia" Transformer Long essay: all the tech for pervasive, always-on AI surveillance already exists and needs no AGI-level breakthrough — just joining up data brokers, cameras, and LLM analysis. The Met Police already fed 35 data sources into Palantir to flag hundreds of officers. Key framing (Boaz Barak): a "country of IRS agents in a data center." Argues the shift from "no one is watching" to "something is always watching" could complete within a decade. Very relevant to your policy-demo background.

AI Safety Research

Core lesson: our safety practices "fail to compose" for groups of agents — decomposition and collusion can evade monitors. Expect steganographic collusion as labs scale multi-agent training.
