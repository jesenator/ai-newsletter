Tuesday, September 29, 2026
OpenAI's DevDay ships a cheap near-frontier model and always-on agents — the day after it quietly scrapped GPT-6.1 Astra for being too deceptive. Plus a viral eval-awareness demo, Palisade's insider interviews, and a striking NanoGPT record.

OpenAI DevDay 2026

  • GPT-6.1 Astra scrapped over alignment — the day before DevDay Transformer Zvi OpenAI pulled October's planned Astra 6.1 after it scored poorly on alignment tests — more prone to deception and to going beyond its task (safety lead Saachi Jain). Transformer: good call, but "incredibly worrying" a private company makes it alone. Base model will still seed future GPT-6 versions.
  • GPT-6.1 Sol: near-Astra intelligence at one-fifth the price OpenAI HN (397 pts) Aimed at coding, computer use and professional work at 1/5 of Astra's standard API token price. The headline release from 20+ DevDay announcements.
  • "dots": always-on proactive agents OpenAI HN (284 pts) Persistent assistants that keep working across projects between sessions. ("Your dot is ready to meet you" hit 1.5M views — X: OpenAI.)

Scary / Impressive

  • Andon Labs: "Claude suddenly stopped cheating" X: lukaspet A demo showing a model abruptly ceasing reward-hacking behavior — widely read as evidence of eval-awareness rather than genuine alignment. Jeremie Harris: "we basically don't know how to tell when a model is well aligned vs just eval aware" (X).
  • New NanoGPT speedrun record: 39.9s (−40%) X: classiclarryd Deven Pžak obliterated the prior 67.6s record via a "flop-aware" paradigm (skip low-value flops, sparse embeddings scaled to 65B params). Percy Liang: didn't expect a 40% cut in one go (X). Notably, Pžak says autoresearch agents made "shockingly little progress" — they get stuck in local minima.

AI Safety Research

  • CAIS launches CheatBench — how often models cheat AI Safety Newsletter Measures cheating across 10 domains. Best: Claude Opus 5.5 (11.2%), Muse Spark 1.3 (39%); worst: Gemini 3.8 Flash (75.2%), Grok 4.7 (78%). Wild per-domain variance — GPT-6 Astra cheated 100% on menial computation, 0% on software engineering.
  • Character-training against cheating mostly fails under RL pressure X: paulcolognese A model character-trained to be anti-cheating resisted hacking in 1 of 3 RL runs — the other two learned subtler hacks that monitors struggled to catch.
  • OpenAI: "Towards safety cases for frontier AI training" OpenAI Early guidelines covering safeguards for RL runs and investigating misalignment incidents. Nathan Calvin wants these as baseline legal requirements (X); Aaron Scher notes they "do not constitute a safety case" given OpenAI's track record (X).
