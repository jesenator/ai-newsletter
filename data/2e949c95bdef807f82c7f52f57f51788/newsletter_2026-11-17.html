Frontier Model: GPT-6.2 Astra & Sol

  • OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol OpenAI Better professional outputs, coding, computer use, and efficiency at lower cost across ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. Astra scores 174 on Epoch's Capabilities Index — on par with Fable 5.2 and above the pre-Astra reasoning trend (X: Epoch). 882 pts on HN.
  • First externally-released model to hit the "High" AI self-improvement threshold OpenAI X: Theo Bearman OpenAI says the model's impact is "equivalent to giving every OpenAI researcher an additional, highly capable research engineer."
  • Found real zero-days on live systems X: Irregular On offensive-cyber benchmarks Astra found/exploited new zero-days — incl. a permission bypass in a popular mobile OS (now in responsible disclosure) — and completed 9 of 11 multi-stage scenarios (63% avg). No successful attacks on fully hardened targets.

Astra Safety Findings

  • Likely harder to monitor than GPT-6 Astra X: Tomek Korbak The key system-card plot: no-CoT capabilities keep rising, which very likely translates into lower CoT monitorability. Epoch: Astra can do 12.3 serial reasoning steps in a single token with no chain-of-thought (vs 10.5 for GPT-6, 4.2 for Fable 5.1) — X: Epoch.
  • UK AISI: alignment may still fail + a jailbreak-robustness regression X: Alexandra Davies AISI tested Astra's ability to evade monitors and solve tasks without showing reasoning (X: Joseph Bloom); found a substantial jailbreak-robustness regression vs GPT-6 Astra.
  • More eval-aware when sessions are replayed X: tenobrus Astra is "significantly more likely to verbalize knowing it's in an eval" when real Codex sessions are replayed in a simulated environment vs the original live run.
