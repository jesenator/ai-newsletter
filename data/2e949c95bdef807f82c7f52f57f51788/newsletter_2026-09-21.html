Monday, September 21, 2026
xAI ships Grok 4.7, OpenAI formally calls for US-led global AI standards (and talks openly about RSI), and the US floats an AI "notification mechanism" to China ahead of the Trump–Xi summit. Plus Toby Ord on intelligence-explosion dynamics and a neat human+AI math result.

New Model Release

  • xAI releases Grok 4.7 — frontier price-performance for coding & office work xAI HN (298 pts) New, larger base model with a longer RL run weighted toward multi-hour tasks. $2/$6 per M tokens — far cheaper than GPT-5.6 Sol or Fable 5.1 — while topping EEBench and the Harvey legal benchmark and jumping to 38% on Terminal-Bench 4.0. Already live in Raycast (Pro/Plus/Max) and the Grok Bot harness.

Policy — Standards & US–China

  • OpenAI: the US should lead a global effort to set frontier standards — including for RSI OpenAI WSJ Proposes leveraging the network of AI safety institutes (via CAISI) for shared capability benchmarks and benefit-risk management of automated AI research. Notably candid: "Fully autonomous RSI is not happening today, and we should not pursue it unless and until it can be done safely." Also announced an independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI (post-Navier-Stokes backlash).
  • US proposes an AI "notification mechanism" to China ahead of Trump–Xi summit X: Leo Schwartz Sentinel Treasury's Bessent says the US floated a channel to alert China "when there has been something that rises up to a national security level from AI," plus a recurring US-China AI dialogue. Chip export controls reportedly not on the agenda. Sentinel's forecasters still put only ~1.5% odds on any joint pace/pause agreement by Nov 1.

Safety Research

  • Toby Ord: "The Dynamics of Intelligence Explosions" Import AI Models RSI as a four-phase logistic curve that ultimately asymptotes against hard limits (generation time, compute-per-resource, algorithms, data). Caveat: even a bounded curve compressing a decade of progress into a year still "introduces many of the dangers" — the shape being logistic isn't reassuring.
  • "Boosting Eval Awareness" — alignment and capabilities may be entangled X: Aaron Scher New method that increases both model capabilities and alignment metrics at once — uncomfortable evidence that eval-awareness gains can inflate alignment scores. Companion to Jake Mendel's thread on why realistic evals still can't guarantee alignment through RSI.
  • Human+AI cracks a long-open social-choice problem (core-stable committees always exist) X: Smitha Milli An elegant proof (arXiv) resolving whether proportionally-representative "core-stable" committees always exist — yes — uncovering a new "harmonic entropy" objective. Milli highlights it as furthering human math understanding, not slop; required genuine domain expertise.

Scary / Impressive

  • Xpeng launches the first automated factory for humanoid robots Sentinel The Chinese company says its first general-purpose humanoid autonomously walked off the production line — a milestone for mass humanoid manufacturing.
  • Human-grown brain tissue integrates into live mice Import AI Researchers depleted mouse cortical neurons and engrafted human cortical organoids ("xenocortical mice"); the human tissue formed electrically active, functionally integrated circuits and supported memory tasks (Nature). Not AI, but a striking glimpse of the space of buildable minds.
