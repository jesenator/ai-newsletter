  • Hendrycks: "eigenism" as an alternative to pure AI control X: Dan Hendrycks New paper argues control-only strategies are limited and a stable "mutualistic" human–AI future may be possible; if AIs are eigenist (interests carried through similar AIs, not a single ego), shutdown-resistance should be milder. 13-point distillation: "you are a pattern, not a vessel." Provocative framing; worth a skeptical read.
