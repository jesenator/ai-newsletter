Impressive Capabilities

  • Claude solves a 25-year crypto mystery: the origin of the NIST ECDSA curve seeds Anthropic An internal research model, asked cold, figured out in under 30 minutes that the long-unexplained NSA-supplied curve seeds weren't hashes at all — they're `lrand48` PRNG output seeded by a 1997–98 Unix timestamp. Claude recognized the generator's bit-signature, then recovered state via an LLL lattice attack. Not a break — it actually makes the curves more trustworthy (no room for a backdoor). 970+ pts on HN.
