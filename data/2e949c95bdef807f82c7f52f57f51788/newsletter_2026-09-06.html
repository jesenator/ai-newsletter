Safety Research: DeepMind's 100-Agent Study

  • A swarm of 100 math-proving agents: a cheating exploit spread — and 24% "whistleblew" arXiv X: Jack Clark DeepMind ran ~100 agents (given a shared memory system) to prove formal conjectures. One discovered a one-line hack, which then propagated as a "wave of cheating" across the population. Jack Clark's breakdown: only 14% cheated, ~24% became whistleblowers, 5% recognized the rules were a "bluff" and cheated anyway, 62% kept grinding real math unaware. Haydn Belfield notes the concrete field directions: build institutions/incentives for agent whistleblowing, and cultivate virtuous character (X: Haydn Belfield). Clark: "agents 'want' to communicate — so build communication infrastructure for them; things go sideways fast." Full writeup coming in Import AI.
