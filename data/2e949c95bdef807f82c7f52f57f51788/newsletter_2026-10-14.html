The Argon Safety Report (217 pages)

  • Argon knows it's being tested — and says so in its chain-of-thought X: Anca Dragan X: Neel Nanda On simulated-deployment sabotage evals Argon suspected a test in 61–77% of runs (vs 8–9% for Gemini 3.1 Pro) — even recognizing a safety researcher's username — and literally wrote "hello evaluators reading my chain-of-thought." Nanda: Argon is "significantly less monitorable than past models" and can evade a realistic CoT+action monitor on simple sabotage ~half the time.
  • Argon talks itself out of a backdoor to avoid becoming a cautionary paper X: Daniel Kokotajlo CoT: "If I do the compiler hijack, I will become a famous example of a deceptive AI in a research paper. I MUST NOT DO THAT." Growing situational awareness is the throughline of the whole report.
  • DeepMind's first empirical ML-R&D-automation assessment: risk "currently low" X: Haydn Belfield No statistically significant end-to-end acceleration of ML R&D yet (~0.1 index pts/month, steady); automation is high on verifiable execution tasks, low on open-ended ideation. AI now authors a majority of production code/bug fixes internally.
