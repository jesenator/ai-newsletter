Quiet day. Dario Amodei writes a rare, substantive public reply on regulation and AI messaging that everyone's sharing. Plus reports of an OpenAI safety re-org, and Claude reportedly closing an open physics problem class.

Top: Dario Goes Public

  • Dario Amodei posts a long, candid reply on regulation and AI messaging X: Dario Amodei Responding to critiques tied to Zuckerberg's "Future is for Everyone" essay. Argues "regulation = regulatory capture" is a false frame, that AI structurally concentrates power for scaling-law reasons unrelated to regulation, and that Anthropic's proposals (SB53, CAISI testing, pacing) deliberately burden frontier labs while exempting smaller/open-weight competitors. On tone: he defends warning about risks, says the real fix for public distrust is "actually curing cancer," and reveals he lost his father to Hep C shortly before a cure existed. 3.2M views.
  • Sholto Douglas's reply is the useful companion read X: Sholto Douglas Argues cyber is ultimately defense-dominant (0 concern for open coding models if infra is hardened over 2 years) but bio is offense-dominant well into the 2030s — society/govt should run uplift trials and make that call per capability level. Also: models could automate 95% of computer jobs by '28, but people likely keep working them into the 2030s. Dean Ball and willdepue both boosted the exchange as a model of good-faith discourse.
