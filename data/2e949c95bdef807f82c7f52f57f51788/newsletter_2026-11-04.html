AI Safety Research

  • Anthropic: FlashProve — practical proofs of which model produced an output Anthropic HN (129) Cryptographic (zkSNARK-style) verification that a response came from a given model — no trusted hardware. Proves a 400B model at >100 tok/s on one H100. A real building block for international AI verification/pacing ("check that hardware serves inference, not training") and anti-hijacking. Open-sourced.
  • Forethought: three types of automated alignment work Forethought Distinguishes empirical alignment research, conceptual/judgment-driven work, and direct alignment of specific systems — a useful taxonomy for reasoning about which parts of alignment AIs can safely automate.
  • A heart image flips Claude/GPT's coding strategy from 0%→100% recursive X: scychan_brains Showing models an irrelevant heart picture makes them switch coding strategies entirely — and they insist the image was irrelevant. Another data point on how alien and poorly-understood model behavior is.
  • Scaling laws from models that score zero X: Percy Liang Introducing a "hint level" turns a benchmark into graded easier versions, letting you jointly fit Accuracy(scale, hint) and forecast GSM8K accuracy within 3 pts from 5600× less compute.
