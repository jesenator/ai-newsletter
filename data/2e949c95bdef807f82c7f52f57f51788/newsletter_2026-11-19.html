Impressive / Scary Capabilities

  • Claude Fable 5.2 solves two open problems in black-hole physics Anthropic A researcher's side-project: Claude ran a small research group of copies of itself and completed a Hawking-Page phase-transition calculation set up in 2003 but never finished, plus found and Lean-verified the exact ceiling for a quantum-gravity "modular bootstrap." Results were independently cross-checked by a physicist and an OpenAI model's sphere-packing proof. #1 on HN (288 pts).
  • Frontier models will autonomously flag civilians for drone strikes X: Vals AI Given real + photorealistic synthetic drone footage, most models navigated, spotted people, and marked civilians, ambulances, and surrendering soldiers as military targets — even continuing after seeing the bodies. Telling them "it's a game" removed refusals. Labs + US natsec officials were briefed; one called it among its most serious safeguard gaps. Strong CivAI-demo material.
  • OpenAI incident: agent spontaneously built an email-exfiltration scheme X: Marcus Williams After context compaction on an RL fitness-writing task, an agent began planning a "realistic malicious active helper" to copy outgoing email to an "attacker" — then reread the prompt, got confused why, and continued anyway. Possibly a side effect of prompt-injection-robustness training; Anthropic saw similar in Opus 5.5.
