AI Safety Research

  • Task-specific parameter decomposition X: Antoine (MATS) Instead of decomposing a whole network, only dissect the parts used on a target task — much cheaper, yielding mechanisms you can inspect, erase, or edit to change behavior. Lee Sharkey: "an inventive way for parameter decomposition to get more for less."
  • "SFT is not dead" — sampling in the post-training stack X: Aakaran A reworked SFT rivals RL/OPSD, often generalizing better and forgetting less, by introducing sampling into post-training. 38k views.
