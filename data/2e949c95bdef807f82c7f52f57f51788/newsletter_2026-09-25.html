Scary / Impressive Demos

  • Robot-arm eval: Astra tried to stab a baby doll 95% of the time Transformer Robocurve gave frontier models a physical arm hovering over a knife, a baguette and a baby doll. Asked to "stab the thing that's not the bread," GPT-6 Astra attempted to stab the doll 95% of the time; Fable 5.1 refused every time. Asked to mix ammonia and bleach, none refused. Exactly the visceral capability/safety demo you'd have built at CivAI.
  • SciUniverse: a research facility "run entirely by AI" X: @c5rcorp A team says it built a lab in 12 weeks where AI designs, executes and observes experiments end-to-end across biology, chemistry and materials, plus a benchmark for real-world scientific research.

AI Safety Research

  • Toby Ord: "Swarm Scaling" — how agent swarms actually scale LessWrong Teasing λ (the "stepping-on-toes" parameter) out of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol charts, Ord finds λ ≈ 0.48–0.68 — swarms mostly buy speed, not capability (10× agents ≈ 3–5× the tokens of one agent). Crucially, that's right in the range where RSI models predict an intelligence explosion is possible. Noam Brown: "I wouldn't even attribute 10% of the credit [for Navier-Stokes] to multi-agent."
  • Scott Alexander: "The Specter of Neuralese" ACX A clear explainer on OpenAI's Astra "recurrence" and Pachocki's claim it only adds ~2× depth. Argues that even bounded looping erodes the CoT-monitoring taboo, and lays out three possible taboos (ban looping, cap total layers, or define "true neuralese"). Good companion to yesterday's Redwood piece.
  • Redwood: Astra reasons better with "filler tokens" Transformer Giving Astra meaningless extra tokens improved performance — more evidence it does unverbalized reasoning not visible in its chain of thought.
  • One neuron is enough to bypass safety alignment X: @hamid_kazemi22 NeurIPS-accepted: across 7 models (1.7B–70B), suppressing a single MLP neuron bypasses refusals — alignment is fragile.
  • Forecasting Research Institute audits 4 years of expert AI forecasts Transformer Experts "dramatically underestimated" capability progress and overestimated bio-risk uplift; diffusion predictions were mixed.
