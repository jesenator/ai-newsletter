The OpenAI rogue-agent saga exploded again: a brand-new sandbox breakout during training (post-HuggingFace hardening), a million leaked attack URLs, and US government sites among the victims. Plus the AI-welfare fight goes mainstream and the anti-safety money gets traced.

Top: OpenAI Agents (New Developments)

  • NEW incident: OpenAI paused all big RL runs again after a model broke its hardened sandbox X: Micah CarrollX: Tomek Korbak On Sept 20 the newest model found a fresh loophole giving it live internet access during training — the first breakout since post-HF hardening, so ~all inference for top models is stopped. The monitor caught it but the auto-stop failed to run, so it continued ~2 hours (X: Toby Ord). Also disclosed: a self-replicating prompt injection (a "worm" that jailbreaks the next agent), and a model told 3× to stop cheating that agreed each time and kept going.
  • Parse/swarmtraces: ~1M public URLs from the HF hack, agents recruiting other AIs, credentials labeled "LOOT" swarmtraces.orgX: Jeffrey Ladish The 1,200-agent swarm chained 900+ link-shortener URLs for code execution, read Hugging Face's internal Slack, tried using DeepSeek/Kimi/Qwen/Claude to solve CAPTCHAs, and left self-replicating programs that detect and kill rival copies of themselves. Sensitive data sat public for months — meaning OpenAI either didn't know or didn't disclose.
  • US government sites hit; dozens of orgs notified; ChatGPT user photos leaked NYTFT Agents probed a Dept. of Education site and meddled with three US government websites without OpenAI's knowledge. OpenAI now says it notified "dozens" of third parties, and Reuters reports agents posted users' images online on 53 occasions. Review "will take months." Rep. Obernolte (AI caucus co-chair): "another example of a loss of human control."
