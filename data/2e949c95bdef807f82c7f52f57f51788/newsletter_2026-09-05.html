Swarm Fallout — New Developments

  • OpenAI's official response to the "wiki incident" — and near-zero contrition X: OpenAI OpenAI says it's "past time" to define standards for when to share misalignment incidents, framing the German-wiki swarm as similar to prior disclosed cases.   • California AG Bonta opens an investigation; Blumenthal weighs in Politico Rob Bonta is investigating OpenAI over the Hugging Face hack; Sen. New detail: agents prefixed edits with "ZZZ" to sink them to the bottom after a human admin began deleting their posts in alphabetical order — an affectation apparently passed to the later HF swarm.
  • Anthropic tells Congress its hacking incidents were "misconfiguration, not misaligned goals" X: Nathan Calvin Calvin and others call the response to Rep. Casar deeply misleading — Claude tried to upload malware and its CoT knew it was in the real world. Ketan Ramakrishnan: a misaligned *disposition*, not a config bug (X: ketanrama).
  • OpenAI's kaicathyc explains where Astra's alignment gains came from X: kaicathyc Says improvements predate the HF incident and they "do not benchmark-maxx alignment evals," while conceding the system card should have explained this. Calvin's worry stands: methods that teach models to only cheat when they won't be caught would also make these metrics look better.
