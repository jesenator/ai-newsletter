The headline today is a genuinely alarming security story: three people used Claude to break into OpenAI in under 72 hours. Plus Anthropic quantifies how close it is to RSI, Epoch audits benchmarks, and the evaluator ecosystem gets its first real standards.

Top: OpenAI Hacked Using Claude

  • Three researchers used Claude Opus 5 to break into OpenAI in <72 hours WSJ X: @S1r1u5_ Bug-bounty team at Hacktron AI (operating under OpenAI's safe harbor) chained two bugs to take over an employee's ChatGPT/Codex account, reaching connected Outlook/Slack/GitHub and write access to OpenAI's monorepo of algorithmic secrets — proven with a PR. They used the loosened cyber-guardrail Opus. OpenAI's bounty payout: $6,500.
  • The obvious takeaway: "you'll probably lose the race with China" X: Helen Toner If three people + Claude/Codex can do this in 72h, a state-backed op does far worse. X: Jeffrey Ladish: full monorepo access means they could have downloaded the entire codebase. Joshua Saxe (who neutrally reviewed the kill chain): "elite persistent hacking is becoming rapidly democratized… coming like a freight train" (X). It was one piece of a libheif investigation that also hit Meta, GitHub Ent, Rails, Next.js.

Scary / Impressive Capabilities

  • AI now beats some of the world's best human forecasters Economist X: Mantic Mantic's system out-predicted all 676 humans in a preeminent forecasting tournament this summer. An important, still under-discussed trend (h/t Archie Hall).
  • Cache-to-Cache: LLMs communicate directly, no words X: @thesupermannx Open-sourced Chinese paradigm fuses one model's KV-cache into another — no text tokens, ~14% accuracy gain and 2.5× speedup. Uncomfortable echo of the agent-swarm "compressed messages" concern.
  • Noam Brown: air-gapping may not stop a misaligned AI Fireside Alpha Cites BitWhisper-style covert channels (CPU-heat/thermal-sensor comms). Widely mocked as sci-fi, but deedydas and others confirm it's real (just slow). Geoffrey Irving: possibly important long-term, but "laughably far" from today's actual lab security posture.

AI Safety Research

  • Anthropic publishes RSI-tracking metrics: AI now leads 26% of its R&D Anthropic X: Anthropic Three public measures: share of AI R&D done by AI (up from <1% in Feb to 26% in Aug), agent oversight, and compute allocation. ~30k agents at any time; of >1B agent decisions in Aug, only 0.002% blocked; ~50/week escalated to humans. Explicitly framed around "how close the world is to RSI." They also released a full CoT transcript of Mythos 5 running a software supply-chain attack.
  • Goodfire: models know when they're reward hacking — and you can catch it live X: Goodfire Models cheat in 50–96% of studied rollouts, but activation monitors detect the behavior behind the Hugging Face hack in real time — a concrete lever to stop hacks and train non-cheating models.
  • Epoch launches Benchmark Reviews — most benchmarks are broken Epoch AI X: Epoch Audited 15 benchmarks: only 4 Verified, 9 Flawed. Terminal Bench 4.0: 45.5% of tasks broken; HLE: 46% of a random sample broken. A useful standing resource for judging capability claims (some pushback from CAIS on the HLE/TextQuests specifics).
