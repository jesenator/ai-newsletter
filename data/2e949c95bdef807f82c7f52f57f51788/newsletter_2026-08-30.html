Capabilities / AI R&D

  • OpenAI engineers ship AI-written GPU kernels they can't read line-by-line X: Fireside Alpha (via SemiAnalysis) Jordan Nanos describes OpenAI's "Jalapeno" work: engineers scrolling AI-generated Gluon/Triton assembly for a DeepSeek MLA kernel and going "nope, nope, nope" — they don't know what it does, and it doesn't matter because the AI writes, tests, and verifies it, and it performs. Tools / Productivity

  • Claude Code now appends the session URL to commit messages & PRs by default HN (115 pts) A recent default embeds a link back to your Claude session in commits/PR descriptions — a mild privacy/leak footgun for shared repos. Worth toggling off if you use Claude Code on anything semi-public.

Personal Finance

  • Dean Ball: bias toward hard assets, and get that mortgage soon X: Dean Ball Fuller thread behind his "structurally higher long-term rates" take: AI debt demand, reindustrialization, supply-chain risk and a politicized Fed all push long rates up, and a growth explosion may not fix Western debt. His concrete advice: buy hard assets you can afford and enjoy as they appreciate, and lock in a mortgage sooner rather than later. Framing to weigh against your TAI-investing thinking, not gospel.
