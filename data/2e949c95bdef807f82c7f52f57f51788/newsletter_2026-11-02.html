GPT-6 Astra edits another model's weights by hand, Anthropic's amended S-1 says it may imminently cross its RSI danger threshold, a pretraining-poisoning attack survives clean post-training, and Transformer's AI Power List drops. Plus a brutal new agentic benchmark and the Scott Alexander pause debate.

Scary Capabilities

  • GPT-6 Astra edits another model's weights by hand — "Weight-Space Programming" rikiyat blog HN 1,395 Astra directly changed only 0.016% of Qwen3.5-0.8B's weights — no gradients, no training — fixing reasoning loops and improving math up to 6× and instruction-following 3×. Neel Nanda tempers it ("surprisingly good at old-school mech interp, not spooky"); David Krueger notes this is a plausible shape for RSI, and ilex_ulmus flags interp as inherently dual-use.
  • Pretraining poisoning plants an RCE backdoor that survives clean post-training arXiv X: Jacob Steinhardt New Anthropic paper: poisoning just 0.1% of pretraining data plants a remote-code-execution backdoor in a CLI agent that survives a full SFT→DPO→RL pipeline (92.7% attack success on a 4B agent). Uses "declaration-based" poison (ordinary docs that state a rule) and natural-string triggers (e.g. a file path with a company name). Defense has to live at the action layer — sandboxing, allow-lists, human sign-off.

AI Safety & Policy

  • Anthropic: we may "imminently" cross our RSI danger threshold SEC S-1/A X: Theo Bearman New language on p.29 says internal estimates indicate models may soon meet the RSP threshold for "dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI" (one year of progress ≈ two years of the 2018–24 baseline). Anthropic is weighing RSP updates to require human review/approval in R&D and keep humans in control of frontier model development. Aaron Scher, Miles Brundage et al. noted the framing of x-risk as a business disclosure (77k+ views).
  • Zvi: "Scott Alexander's Against SURA Seems Deeply Confused" Zvi The latest round in the unilateral-pause debate. Scott Alexander says nothing has updated him much since ~2019 on how bad RLVR turns out, and reports that Astra, Fable 5.x and Opus 5.5 all "voted" for an immediate 10+ year pause when he polled them. Related tempers from Nate Soares, Charlie Bullock and a Wharton finance prof (shared by Nathan Calvin).
  • Transformer × NY Mag: The AI Power List Transformer 26 behind-the-scenes people "whose influence far outstrips their fame" — Logan Graham, Sneha Revanur, Dean Ball, Amy Kremer, Misha Laskin, Beth Barnes, Alexander Berger, and more. Drawn from 50+ insider interviews; average age 36, overwhelmingly male.

Benchmarks

  • GDPx: a frontier agentic benchmark where the best model scores 12% X: SemiAnalysis New benchmark measuring agentic capability across economically valuable domains; even the top model gets just 1/7 (12%). 1.4M views; Jaime Sevilla "looks promising if it withstands scrutiny."
  • Realtime-Bench: LLMs are bad at low-latency tasks Import AI 479 50 interactive game/web tasks where delayed actions hurt. Under real wall-clock latency, GPT-6 Astra (high) tops out at 36%; with a paused clock, reasoning models hit 70–80%. A clean proxy for where agents will break (e.g. live auctions, games).
