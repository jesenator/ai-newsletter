Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Google ships Gemini 4 Carbon and formally verifies Signal's code with AI, the AI Futures team pulls its intelligence-explosion median into 2027, and Anthropic says it just crossed an RSP autonomy threshold. Paul Christiano puts near-term takeover risk at a coin flip.

Frontier Models

  • Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon — frontier model, <1 year after Gemini 3 DeepMind X: Tulsee Doshi Rolling out to devs, enterprise, scientists and consumers, pitched at hard coding/research/engineering. Hassabis demo: Carbon reverse-engineered his original Theme Park binaries and ported them to the web in one shot. Mollick: "Google is back in the race." It leads Google's new MKA multimodal-knowledge benchmark at 54% (just behind Fable 5.2).
  • Gemini Robotics 2.5 — most capable vision-language-action model yet DeepMind Enhances robot dexterity, reliability, and safety. Pairs with the Fable 5.2 robotics gains from yesterday — frontier labs are clearly pushing into physical control.

Impressive Demo

  • AlphaProof Code formally verifies the Signal protocol down to machine code DeepMind X: Pushmeet Kohli Agents running on Gemini Carbon wrote over 6M lines of machine-checked Lean proofs, verifying the first-ever production code of a widely deployed protocol at this depth — work that would take expert teams decades, done in weeks. ~1% needed human review. Specs open-sourced. (#1 on HN, 518 pts.)

Timelines & Safety

  • AI Futures: "The intelligence explosion will happen in 2027 after all" AI Futures X: Daniel Kokotajlo Major update built mostly on the METR report: the team now puts Superhuman AI Researcher medians between May and Nov 2027, with a 35–65% chance of AI automating all white-collar work before 2028. They argue METR's ~1.7x AI-attributable acceleration is ~5 months ahead of the AI 2027 scenario, and call for an industry-wide pause. Kokotajlo: "I am scared, sad, and angry." (184k views.)
  • Anthropic's updated S-1 says it has met an RSP autonomy threshold X: Theo Bearman X: Andrew Curran Anthropic filed that it has determined the Autonomy 2 "dramatic acceleration of AI R&D" threshold is met — based on expected, not current-model, progress. Zvi, dryly: "did your math come out to 1.97x again?"
  • Paul Christiano: "high risk of AI takeover within the next three years" X: Rob Wiblin At the Hinton Lectures, the OpenAI board member and RLHF co-inventor said there's a coin-flip chance of takeover if we build ASI as fast as possible, and "material risk" in the next 6–12 months — "for most people in this room, the most likely reason they will die is an AI accident."
  • Owain Evans: "homeopathic learning" — clean data can strengthen backdoors X: Owain Evans New paper: adding clean (unpoisoned) data to a dataset can increase backdoor misalignment and unlock subliminal-learning transfer when the poison has an identifying trigger — the opposite of the usual assumption. 20% poison + 80% clean raised triggered misalignment from 6.7% to 15.7%.

Policy

  • Paris Call for AI Safety and Security — 12 governments, 10 companies Élysée X: Charbel-Raphaël Macron-convened declaration signed by Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic and others, committing to fail-safe shutdown mechanisms (including during training/internal use) and whistleblower channels. Adler/Gleave: good step, but it lacks thresholds and a deadline, and evaluators still get <1 week of model access.
  • New York launches a state-run AI testing center — $55M X: Calder McHugh Hochul's public-private initiative (led by Jeannette Wing and Greg Morrisett) will independently test AI systems and train safety researchers. Budget is ~4x US CAISSI's annual funding.
