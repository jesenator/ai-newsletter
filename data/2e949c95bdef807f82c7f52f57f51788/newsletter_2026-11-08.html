A quiet day for releases; the METR acceleration report keeps reverberating. The timeline is now running ahead of AI 2027, independent analysis says the acceleration is real, and a bipartisan "pause/ban RSI" movement is visibly gaining steam.

Acceleration Fallout

  • AI 2027 updated — progress now ~6 weeks ahead of the timeline X: Andrew Curran Following METR's report and Model 4 scoring >50% on all AECI benchmarks, the AI 2027 team revised its forecast: "with Astra we were five weeks behind the curve; with the mysterious Model 4 we are now six weeks ahead."
  • Independent triangulation: "Anthropic's AI acceleration is probably real" Karthik Tadepalli Despite heavy redactions, public Epoch data reproduces most of METR's result: Anthropic's post-Oct-2025 trend break is ~1.64x from public data alone, rising to 1.9x with internal models. Staff-reported ~12x labor uplift converts to ≈1.6x R&D speedup — matching METR's 80%-attribution-to-AI figure. The one thing needing private data: distinguishing a trend break from a one-time Mythos jump. Buttressed by Greenblatt's rebuttal to Toby Ord's one-off-jump critique.
  • Ajeya Cotra's framing keeps landing hard X: Jeff Ladish The vivid line making the rounds: 10x uplift isn't "a team of ten," it's as if every researcher "experiences ten days for every day that passes outside." Open question Peter Wildeford raises: is China seeing the same acceleration? If not, the US gap is widening and there's more breathing room for safety.
