OpenAI Safety Firings — The Insiders Speak

  • Update: Korbak, Balesni & Wang say they were fired for prioritizing safety letter (PDF) X: Tomek Korbak The three fired researchers (covered Oct 1) have now gone public. Kelsey Piper: "the information is in, and it looks incredibly bad" (X: Kelsey Piper); widespread calls for OpenAI to explain (X: Daniel Kokotajlo).
