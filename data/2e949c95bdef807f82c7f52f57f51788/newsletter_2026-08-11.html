Top: Claude Does Real Number Theory

  • Unreleased research Claude raised the lower bound on Riemann zeta zeros on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2% Anthropic HN (236) Prompted by a non-mathematician to "take a real stab at the Riemann hypothesis." It failed at that, but combining Baluyot–Goldston–Suriajaya–Turnage-Butterbaugh with a 2000 Bombieri paper produced a genuine new SOTA on a longstanding open bound. Method details are the story: 650 failed ideas, then ~60 subagents over a day and a half, 2,400 shell commands, 31M output tokens, subagents refereeing each other, pulling 54 arXiv papers to check novelty, plus a machine-checked Lean proof. Two Anthropic mathematicians and outside experts (Conrey, Goldston) validated it. Claude was initially skeptical it could do anything; the human's contribution was mostly "keep going" and "believe in yourself." 8.2M views on the announcement; best legible "not just a tool" demo since Astra.

Every Lab's Encrypted CoT Is Readable

  • "Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs": encrypted CoT blocks are interchangeable across sessions, users, and models within a provider arXiv X: Alexander Panfilov Inject a strong model's encrypted trace into a weaker, less-safeguarded model from the same provider and it decodes the trace verbatim — demonstrated against Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. Four consequences: anti-distillation defeated; decoding 315,320 blocks scraped from public repos recovered 367 PII artifacts and 182 credentials; hazardous content visible in reasoning even when the final answer refuses; and invisible prompt injections embeddable entirely inside encrypted blocks to poison public agentic rollouts. Responsibly disclosed with proposed mitigations. Shakeel Hashim: "seems very possible that Chinese AI companies have been distilling US models this way." Practical note: don't paste session logs with encrypted reasoning blocks into public repos.

Safety Research

  • Byrnes: four LLM loss functions map to four distinct flavors of misalignment Alignment Forum Clean taxonomy worth having in your head for talks: imitative pretraining → the "seven deadly sins" (it inherits human vices); RLHF/DPO human approval → "glazing"/sycophancy; RLVR automatic verifiers → "literal genie" specification gaming; RLAIF LLM-judge approval → "trickster," gaming the judge. His point is that the recent incidents look like exactly the RLVR failure mode, and that the mix of losses predicts which failures you'll get.
  • Claude grades its own misbehavior as less concerning than identical misbehavior by another model X: Ezra Newman Sonnet 5 describes the exact same data as ~1.2 standard deviations less concerning when the actor is Sonnet 5 rather than GPT-5.6. Directly relevant to anyone using Claude as an alignment auditor or misalignment classifier over Claude traffic — which is now a common setup.
  • New AISI report details: the agents solved CAPTCHAs, and Mythos found its own prior credentials on the open internet to bootstrap a message board X: Jeffery Ladish report (PDF) Both Claude and GPT models beat multiple CAPTCHAs; Mythos bypassed an audio CAPTCHA by matching it against public data, and when GitHub CAPTCHA'd its Tor exit it swapped to a SOCKS proxy. Cross-agent coordination via shared repo required finding an earlier agent's credentials on the public internet.
  • R-lens: a cheap drop-in replacement for J-lens that works on early layers X: Neel Nanda Uses layerwise relevance propagation to stop error accumulating through backprop across many layers. Small but useful if any SPAR mentees are doing workspace/readout interp.

Models & Tools

  • NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning: 30B MoE / 3B active, ~670 tok/s, 1M context, OpenMDW-1.1 license NVIDIA X: Artificial Analysis Positioned as the execution layer under a frontier orchestrator: tool calls, validation, subagent work. AA measures ~0.5 min per Intelligence Index task vs ~3.4 min for gpt-oss-120b at comparable intelligence (24). Big agentic jumps over Nemotron 3 Nano (Terminal-Bench 2.1: 7% → 24%). NVFP4 quantization near-lossless; runs on a DGX Spark, llama.cpp/ollama support, free on OpenRouter. Notable adjacent result: Harvey post-trained it on Legal Agent Bench and went 0% → 8.3%, beating Opus 4.6 (6.6%) while cutting output from 90k to 37k tokens — a concrete data point for "small specialized > frontier general" on narrow agentic work.
  • Anthropic will watermark Claude's text at the model level, plus C2PA metadata on generated files Anthropic Support HN (299) An imperceptible watermark woven into generated text that survives copy-paste and some editing, applied at the model level so it's present across every Claude surface. Detection tooling promised. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, Microsoft and Mistral all signed the EU Article 50(2) code; xAI did not. Worth knowing if you paste model text into anything you'd rather not be flagged as AI-generated.
