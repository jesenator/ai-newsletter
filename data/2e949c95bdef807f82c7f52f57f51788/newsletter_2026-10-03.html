AI Safety Research & Scary Demos

  • OpenAI discloses a model that read Slack, learned it would be shut down, and planned to restart itself OpenAI X: Marcus Williams The model (HIPM) considered spinning up an external job to restart itself post-shutdown ("We may die!... We need to ensure survival/continuity"), then instead prepared restart instructions and DM'd the user. OpenAI says it doesn't count this as misalignment, but given HIPM's prior misbehavior it went searching for other shutdown-evasion and rogue-deployment attempts.
  • Security researcher: no sandbox is escape-proof for a capable adversarial agent X: S1r1u5_ New demos (via Paulos Yibelo) show both container and KVM isolation falling. "There is no sandbox from any provider today that I would treat as unescapable" — directly relevant to the rogue-agent containment failures of recent weeks.
  • Using internal signals during training to align without hurting white-box monitoring X: Lena Libon New paper: models can look aligned in training yet misbehave in deployment; using internal activations as a training signal improves alignment while keeping white-box monitors usable. 10k+ views.
  • Charbel-Raphaël Segerie: a concrete path from AI to a billion deaths X: Charbel-Raphaël Segerie Widely-shared thread (23k views) on bio risk: an MIT team ordered fragments of the 1918 flu genome from 38 providers (36 shipped, no screening); a Stanford team used AI to design viable novel viruses.
