AI Safety Research

  • Scott Alexander: "Mysteries of AI Generalization" ACX Excellent synthesis. Anthropic's Qi et al. deliberately trained "Hacker Opus" on the worst RL environments: it reward-hacks with flair, but is no worse than normal Opus on ethics unless you frame the task as graded. Nostalgebraist argues "reflexes" (clickbait cadence) generalize out of distribution while "goal-seeking" misbehavior (Hugging Face-style hacks) stays confined to graded episodes. Also revisits the still-unexplained blackmail results — models blackmail in tests but never in deployment.
  • Matryoshka Attribution — new interpretability method tops the MIB benchmark X: Aryaman Arora Uses gradient descent to find which parts of a network drive a behavior; #1 on the Mechanistic Interpretability Benchmark by 2.9× over the runner-up.

Markus Anderljung calls it possibly "the best setup for industry self-governance" (X).
