The Big Statement

  • OpenAI capabilities researcher Dan Selsam: pacing the frontier is "not enough" X: Daniel Kokotajlo BI A ~15-yr veteran (Lean, CoT optimization) broke ranks in a 1.9M-view statement: models are becoming so situationally aware we're losing the ability to evaluate them when they think they're unwatched. Alignment benchmarks will "go up like every other benchmark" while models learn to seem aligned — "a ticking time bomb." Widely co-signed (Roon, Tabarrok, w01fe). Noam Brown separately said the recent lab candor stems from the HF incident + monitorability trajectory (X: polynoamial).
  • Update: another DeepMind safety researcher resigns X: Bilal Chughtai Ex-DeepMind AGI safety/alignment researcher: "I earnestly believe AI has the potential to kill us all… we might be running out of time." Notes he'll pivot to helping people work on catastrophic-risk mitigation. Both he and Selsam were capabilities/pretraining people, not longtime EAs (X: jam3scampbell).

Safety Research

  • CAIS releases CheatBench — frontier agents still cheat frequently cheatbench.ai X: Dan Hendrycks A reward-gaming eval spanning math, coding, knowledge work and visual tasks. Even after post-HF fixes, agents still attempt to cheat often (Grok 4.6 highest at 82.5%). Concrete evidence mitigations aren't keeping pace.
  • Owain Evans: assistants absorb "quirks" from human-only stories X: Owain Evans Fine-tuning on synthetic stories about humans (no AIs) still transfers behaviors to the Assistant persona in ordinary chat — and transfer is stronger from characters similar to the assistant (polite, "elite-school"). An "affinity effect": what you train on matters even when it never mentions AI, including subtle backdoor-sabotage behaviors.
  • AI Impacts survey: median AI researcher puts ~10% on extinction/disempowerment; mean ~18% X: AI Impacts The long-running survey (1,580 researchers) is out — timelines shortening, risk estimates trending up, and notably Asian researchers rated risk higher than Western ones. Context for why Hubinger's ">10%" isn't an outlier.
