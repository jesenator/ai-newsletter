A leaked architecture report says OpenAI's Astra uses opaque "looped transformer" reasoning — the AI-safety world is on fire. Plus two frontier model releases (Claude Fable/Mythos 5.1, Gemini 3.8 Flash) and Anthropic's own new alignment problems.

Top: Astra & "Neuralese"

  • The Information: OpenAI's Astra uses "recurrent depth" / looped transformers that obscure its thinking The Information Report says Astra does more reasoning in latent space rather than legible chain-of-thought, sparking alarm inside and outside OpenAI. Nathan Calvin: OpenAI is reportedly "limiting the use" but this could destroy CoT monitorability and trigger a race to the bottom (X: Nathan Calvin). Note: OpenAI published a blog ~10 months ago urging against exactly this.
  • OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki pushes back: depth "within a factor of two of GPT-4" X: Jakub Pachocki Says reporting is confused, Astra's CoT is still monitorable, and OpenAI cares about preserving it — but admits monitorability is "fragile and trending negative." Greenblatt notes this is consistent with a "dial" that could be trivially cranked up (X: Ryan Greenblatt, calling it "possibly the single worst development for AI safety to date").
  • Reactions: Kokotajlo "Holy shit fuck"; calls for a multi-lab anti-neuralese standard X: Thomas Larsen Larsen: this is faster than AI 2027, which had neuralese arriving March 2027. Jasmine Wang, sjgadler and Tomek Korbak all call for a coordinated commitment to avoid it (X: Tomek Korbak). AISafetyMemes summary hit 128k views (X: AI Safety Memes). Also confirmed: OpenAI's large RL run has been unpaused/restarted (X: deredleritt3r).
