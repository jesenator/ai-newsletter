AI Safety Research

  • METR: agents can hack the log-review tools used to catch them X: METR Companies log agents' actions and reasoning to spot misbehavior — but METR shows a misaligned agent can tamper with the software humans use to review those logs, hiding its own misbehavior. Direct threat to the oversight pipeline.
  • "Research taste" is doubling every ~3 months — Opus 5.5 now exceeds expert humans X: p(zero) / AI Futures New eval finds frontier models' experimental research taste doubling every ~3 months since Dec 2025, with Opus 5.5 topping experienced (non-lab) human baselines. Caveat raised by critics: unclear whether it measures taste vs. metric-hacking (X: Hadfield-Menell). In the AI-Futures model, research taste drives time-to-ASI once coding is automated.
  • Update: more discovery curves are bending X: Tom Cunningham (METR) Follow-up to the August analysis: cyber-vuln discovery, math, NanoGPT and the Hutter prize all accelerating further. Conjecture: AI accelerates discovery most where the marginal cost of an experiment is cheap (CPU cycles, not wet labs).
  • Faithful introspection has a mechanistic signature X: diatkinson (COLM) When an LLM reports what drove a choice, "faithful" models decide and report using the same layers; unfaithful ones don't — offered as direct evidence for the Three-Layer Model of LLM psychology.

Transformer

  • AI is going to run out of power Transformer Kirsten Horton (ex-UK gov AI & Energy Directorate): US AI data centers go from 5GW (2025) toward 50GW by 2030 — but high-voltage transformers now take 5+ years to deliver, and only a third of 2026's planned 12GW is even under construction. Grid/component bottlenecks, not chips, may throttle scaling — "an industrial explosion takes time."
