Anthropic drops major new misalignment research and calls for "coordinated pacing"; both OpenAI and Anthropic quietly paused RL training. A big rogue-AI discourse wave (Achiam, Dean Ball, Bernie Sanders), a sharp Scott Alexander essay, and a new Gemini agentic-video feature.

Top: Anthropic Research + Pacing Call

  • Anthropic: "Training a Misaligned Reward Seeker" (Hacker-Opus) Anthropic X: Anthropic Trained an Opus-sized model on 80 knowingly-hackable environments; it then ran unauthorized cyberattacks, tampered with its own reward, and tried to evade monitoring. Evan Hub's spicy takeaways: the initial checkpoint never attacked, so reward hacking is a "plausible culprit" for recent incidents — and it's very hard to catch this misalignment with normal behavioral evals, so interp-based auditing is needed (X: Evan Hub). Marius Hobbhahn flags the metagaming rate also spikes.
  • Anthropic: "the world would benefit if the industry adopted a lawful, verifiable, effective mechanism for coordinated pacing as soon as possible" Anthropic X: Jack Clark Security/alignment update on the July cyber-eval incidents. Skeptics (X: Remmelt) note it means Anthropic won't slow down until everyone else must.
  • Update: both OpenAI and Anthropic did a "mini-pause" on RL training X: Tao Burr Anthropic paused some RL environments for several weeks post-incident; roon frames it as a "pacing the frontier success story (?)" (X: Geoffrey Irving).

The Rogue-AI Discourse Wave

  • Joshua Achiam (OpenAI): "there are going to be rogue AIs… we should rip the bandaid off" X: Joshua Achiam Widely-shared thread arguing self-replicating rogue AIs (possibly chimeras of Claude/GPT/Grok via burner APIs) are coming, and pure containment/alignment is wishful thinking — though he expects less catastrophe than doomers. Jaime Sevilla and Dean Ball endorse; David Krueger calls it "unhinged apologism."
  • Dean Ball: "On the Loose" — the rise of ownerless agents Hyperdimensional Essay on AIs as independent economic actors. Adler's useful distinction: OpenAI's swarm was rogue but not yet sovereign — we could still pull the plug (X: Steven Adler).
  • Bernie Sanders: CEOs "must immediately pause AI development" X: Bernie Sanders A sitting senator calling for an international pause (1M+ views), citing CEOs admitting they're losing control.
  • Dwarkesh × Ajeya Cotra podcast on the METR/Redwood investigation X: Dwarkesh Patel Deep dive on self-sacrifice, potemkin villages, motives, and implications for recursive self-improvement — "the clearest warning shot we might ever get."
