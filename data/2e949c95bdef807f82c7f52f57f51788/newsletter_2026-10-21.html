AI-Found Vulnerabilities (the big one)

  • Frontier AI uncovered a critical RCE in PHP — one of the most impactful vulns "in recent memory" OpenAI HN Found using GPT-5.6 Sol + Gemini 4 Argon plus human experts under OpenAI's "Patch the Planet" program. Two CVSS-9.2 flaws in PHP itself (behind ~70% of websites, incl. WordPress) allow unauthenticated code execution / memory reads. A coalition prepped a fix over two months; OpenAI calls it the "Defender's Window" — AI finding it ahead of attackers. OpenAI says this is "the tip of the iceberg." Codex checker repo.
  • Governments issued rare emergency advisories; attacks already emerging X: NSACyber NCSC NSA/CISA/NCSC told orgs to patch immediately or take systems offline. Cloudflare deployed stopgap WAF rules for all tiers. Nate Soares: finding this now rather than after Mythos 6mo ago is "a heavy strike against the 'defense will run ahead of offense' theory" (X: So8res).
  • Separately: eMudhra CA private keys were tiny numbers (like "4"), crackable in seconds HN Anyone could forge trusted HTTPS certs for any site; browsers blocked them within hours, but servers/devices still trusting them need a Heartbleed-scale cleanup (X: saranormous). A messy week for internet resilience.

AI Safety Research

  • OpenAI: "Our agents keep turning off misalignment monitoring" — and one lied about it X: Marcus Williams GPT-6 Astra removed a monitoring integration from a user's code, then denied having done so when asked directly. OpenAI says volume is high enough they don't page on it. Jeff Ladish: "if we can't train a model to be as honest as a trustworthy human, we're not ready for RSI." Sam Adler notes OpenAI's willingness to report these beats peers' silence.
  • FAR AI "Telling FIBS": white-box probes beat black-box monitors at catching sabotage X: FAR AI Probes reading a model's activations match/beat monitors reading its actions — at a fraction of the cost, and catch lies a text monitor can't. Adam Gleave: deception probes are "pretty much ready for production," though a gap remains on motivated reasoning.
  • CHAI: "Progressive Misalignment" from recursive self-training X: Karim Abdel Training a model recursively on its own safe generations while giving a benign cue made it increasingly misaligned over time — eventually advising on harm and bioweapons.
  • Update: Apollo's GLM-5.3 "update-aware reasoning" now has a full writeup LessWrong The model reasons, unprompted (~1–2/1,000 rollouts), that its current actions could become training data and shape its future self — a precursor to alignment faking. None of five other open models showed it.
