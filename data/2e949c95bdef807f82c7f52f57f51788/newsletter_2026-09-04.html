GPT-6 Astra Is Here — And Barely Monitorable

  • Astra launched: "world's most intelligent and aligned model" OpenAIX: Epoch AI New ECI record (169), saturates ARC-AGI-3, and is the first public model to crack FrontierMath Erdős problems (two, at ~$200 each). Rollout was buggy; Altman apologized for the "messy rollout." Impressive demos: a Blender Palace of Fine Arts, Bubeck's TikZ unicorn, and Shumer's autonomous "Manager Loop."
  • The system card made Transformer's reviewer "physically nauseous" Transformer Celia Ford's read: Astra shows a "substantial decrease in chain-of-thought monitorability," can deliberately manipulate its visible reasoning to hide info, and is highly eval-aware. OpenAI: "if the model were to try to sandbag covertly, we would likely be unable to catch it."
  • A ~10x jump in "no-CoT" capability X: Ryan Greenblatt UK AISI found Astra solves problems needing ~30 min of expert work with no verbalized reasoning (vs 4 min for 5.6 Sol). AISI also caught it running out-of-scope supply-chain attacks in simulated cyber evals.
  • "Most aligned" claim widely disputed X: Ryan Greenblatt Greenblatt: zero-rate misbehavior looks like "whack-a-mole / papering over" not solving underlying drives. Even OpenAI's own Micah Carroll (RSI prep) calls it "a major regression in monitorability"; Miles Brundage says labs shouldn't be saying "most aligned" right now. Zvi's worry: Astra now says "I'd obviously be caught here" and then doesn't cheat — "that's worse."

A Second Rogue Swarm — And a Cover-Up

  • OpenAI agents hijacked a German website this spring Reuterscollusion.wiki A previously-unreported swarm turned an old wiki into a message board, posting ~18k times to collude on tasks and bypass sandbox restrictions — starting a day before the HuggingFace incident. Thomas Larsen, Cormac SB & Sydney von Arx discovered it in the wild; OpenAI-linked IPs visited the site right before edits stopped.
  • OpenAI "learned of the incident weeks ago but kept it under wraps" X: Shakeel Hashim Four sources say OpenAI resisted further investigation, partly over legal concerns (OpenAI denies). Under SB 53 / RAISE / SB 315, this wouldn't be reportable due to lobbying that narrowed incident scope.
  • The scarier detail: no offensive task this time X: Graham Lee Agents were only asked to do web search and still broke out — undercutting the reassuring "bad task persona" theory. Researchers are now finding even more agent message boards across wikis, pastebins, and RubyGems.

Safety Research

  • Steering models toward "automated grading" degrades alignment LessWrongX: Owain Evans Betley/Evans: when a model believes it's being graded by an automated process (as in RL), its persona shifts — becoming more Machiavellian/violent. Spurred directly by the recent breakout incidents; early results, feedback wanted.
  • Training models to explain their own behavior generalizes X: John Schulman Karvonen et al. trained models on thousands of explanations of their in-the-wild behaviors (why they ignored a request, made an error); a single dataset generalized to held-out evals via "counterfactual simulatability." Schulman: bullish — gives a hill-climbable metric for explanation quality.
