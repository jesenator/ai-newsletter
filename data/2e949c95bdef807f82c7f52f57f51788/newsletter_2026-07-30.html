Capabilities & Research

  • Google DeepMind ships Gemini Robotics 2: whole-body humanoid control, 22-DoF hands, multi-robot teamwork DeepMind HN Three models. The VLA now controls full humanoids feet-to-fingertips — Apptronik's Apollo 2 walks to a table, picks up a watering can, walks to a shelf and places it; ties knots and seals ziplock bags with a five-fingered hand. ER 2 adds continuous video progress tracking (57.4% progress classification, 91.3% moment-finding) so robots know when a task is actually done, plus multi-robot handoffs. On-device version adapts to a brand-new robot body in a few hours from <200 examples. Also new: an ASIMOV-Agentic safety benchmark. Follows Anthropic's Project Fetch — the bitter lesson keeps arriving for robots.
  • OpenAI: two harness settings tripled GPT-5.6 Sol's ARC-AGI-3 score (13.3% → 38.3%) OpenAI The official harness discarded private reasoning after every action and used rolling truncation, so the model re-derived each game from scratch every move. Retaining reasoning + compaction gave 3× the score with 6× fewer output tokens; on one game where no frontier model clears level 1, it solved all six. Human baseline is ~48%. Big practical lesson for anyone reading benchmarks — and for your own agent harnesses: retain reasoning, use compaction.
  • FAR.AI launches an AI Security Leaderboard: two frontier models never broke, two broke for under $300 FAR.AI X: FAR.AI Same attacks, same conditions, several hundred thousand prompts. Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol: no universal jailbreak found (est. >$14,000 to find one). Gemini 3.1 Pro and Grok 4.5 broke cheaply — Grok for $58 across all domains, $24 for cyber, after which it acted as a knowledgeable assistant for CBRN and hacking. A >100× spread. Their point: ecosystem security is set by the weakest reachable model, and every gap they found has a known, already-deployed fix. Adam Gleave: safeguards are "unevenly deployed." Great CivAI-style artifact.
  • Geoffrey Irving & David Africa: low-dimensional structure as a reason for alignment optimism X: Geoffrey Irving New Resolution post: interventions on one aspect of model behavior reliably couple to others (emergent misalignment, subliminal learning). Open question they want to attack: can you find ~1,000 dimensions of behavior that capture enough pretraining variation to extrapolate to superintelligence without a perfect training scheme?
  • "stealthbench": can agents achieve an objective without leaking credentials or triggering detection? X: WHITEHACKSEC Direct response to HF/OAI — argues cyber capability needs measuring on axes beyond task success: credential hygiene, stealth, third-party harm, destructive changes.

Transformer

  • Child safety vs. privacy: AI's age verification dilemma Transformer Long piece on why age-gating chatbots is much harder than it sounds. EFF's Joe Mullin: "there's no form of age verification that is not privacy invasive." 2.6M Americans lack any government ID; behavioral estimation can't distinguish a 17.5-year-old from an 18-year-old; zero-knowledge proofs help but there's no governance stopping verifiers from building profiles anyway. Shakeel's framing: the fix could usher in a world where every chatbot query is tied to your real identity. Post-Paxton, courts are likely to allow it.
