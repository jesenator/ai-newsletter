Satya Nadella publishes a notable essay arguing we should treat AI models as insider risks, UK spy chiefs tell the cabinet AI is the "biggest thing," Bernie Sanders demands AI CEOs be prosecuted, and a new mech-interp thread sharpens the story on model introspection.

Discourse & Policy

  • Satya Nadella: treat frontier models as "insider risks" Nadella essay X: Satya Nadella Widely-shared piece (Musk, Chamath amplifying) arguing we can't attribute model behavior to any code path, so we must "separate the supply of intelligence from the authority over it." Calls for controls outside the model: CoT transparency, model diversity, tamper-proof logging, independent audit, and an "emergency brake." David Kasten: good, but this is containment — it doesn't mean alignment is solved (X: David Kasten).
  • UK spy chiefs tell cabinet AI is a top national-security threat The Times X: Ben Riley-Smith MI5/MI6/GCHQ heads briefed the cabinet Sept 8; McCallum reportedly called AI "the biggest thing you should be thinking about." A summer gov hackathon found 407 vulnerabilities across nine bodies for just £13k. One minister likened the moment to early COVID/2008.
  • Bernie Sanders: prosecute AI CEOs, pause advanced AI now X: Bernie Sanders Riffing on the WSJ coverage of Claude's fake homicide tip / 19 visa apps: "If you or I called in a fake homicide tip… we'd be arrested. The same standard must apply to AI CEOs." 460k views. Separately, a NYT editorial backs strict-liability bills (Bores-NY, Trahan federal draft).
  • Backlash to David Sacks' "alignment produced nothing useful" X: Dean Ball X: Timothy B. Lee Both note RLHF — an alignment technique — is what made conversational chatbots work and kicked off the entire AI boom.

Safety Research

  • Jack Lindsey: a sharper picture of model "introspection" X: Jack Lindsey Detailed thread unpacking Anthropic's injected-thought work: anomaly detection vs. identification are handled by distinct mechanisms; the detection step doesn't scale with capability and is brittle (light elicitation like ablating a refusal vector dramatically boosts it). Models may only "notice" injected thoughts because the prompt primes them to. Ties into the global-workspace paper on which representations are "verbalizable."
  • Ségerie: the marginal value of another safety eval has collapsed X: Charbel-Raphaël Ségerie Of 1,002 traced eval findings (185 about a named lab), ~one led to binding policy action. Argues incidents + capability marketing now give more warning than evals, and public dangerous benchmarks may have accelerated timelines. "The bottleneck is political will, not research." Relevant framing for Kairos talent allocation.
  • Eli Lifland posts current takeoff odds X: Eli Lifland ~10–15% broadly-superhuman AI by EO2027, ~50% by EO2030; conditional on no slowdown, ~67% AI takeover, and ~40% extinction conditional on takeover. 176k views.
