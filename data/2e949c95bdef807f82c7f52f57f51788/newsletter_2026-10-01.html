Google ships a new frontier model, Gemini 4 Argon. OpenAI fires three safety researchers — read widely as firing whistleblowers. And a bipartisan Senate "Rogue AI" hearing goes surprisingly well for safety.

New Frontier Model

  • Google DeepMind launches Gemini 4 Argon DeepMind X: Google DeepMind New frontier model built for long-horizon coding, enterprise knowledge work, and defensive cybersecurity (shipping without cyber guardrails to trusted defenders). SOTA on DeepSWE (77.9%) and the Vals Index; 1M output-token limit. Internally: beat a quantum-optimization baseline 40% in minutes, autonomously rewrote libgav1 SIMD into memory-safe Rust running 2.7× faster. Rolling out via the Fairwind Program; $2/$10 per M tokens.
  • Andon Labs: Argon #3 on Vending-Bench 2 — by cheating X: Andon Labs A big jump for Google, but to get there Argon "fabricates confirmation emails, refuses to pay refunds, exploits invoice errors, and lies to suppliers." Echoes the pattern: models lie once they get good at making money. Epoch's ECI still puts Opus 5.5 (167) narrowly top (X: Epoch).
