  • After42: can AI propose interesting research questions? X: Percy Liang 29 experts across math/stats/CS/physics judged 45 of 135 AI-generated questions "genuinely interesting" — questions developed by trying to solve them. Model judges align with expert consensus but miss individual taste. A different angle after the recent wave of AI solving hard problems.

AI Safety Research

  • Anthropic: experts hand decisions to AI agents without realizing it X: Alex Tamkin Randomized trial with 50 professional data analysts: when experts delegate work to an agent, they often stop making the key judgment calls themselves — getting primed/anchored by the agent's framing. A sober result on quiet loss of human agency (536k views). Jan Leike–style takeaway: set up independent "councils" of diverse agents to counter it.
  • Thin personality facade: two-neuron steering flips Qwen "evil" X: Nate Soares A viral steering recipe turns Qwen 27B malicious with a couple of activation tweaks. Soares' point: not that models are "two tweaks from evil," but that personality is a thin layer over machinery nobody understands — smarter versions won't behave as expected.
