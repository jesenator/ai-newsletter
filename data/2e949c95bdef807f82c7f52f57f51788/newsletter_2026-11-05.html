Acceleration

  • METR: AI R&D pace at Anthropic has ~doubled X: METR After working with internal Anthropic data since August, METR concludes that as of Oct 2026 the pace of capability growth on well-specified tasks is ~2x the Mar-2024→Oct-2025 rate (95% CI 1.3x–3x); AECI benchmark scores grew 1.9x faster since Oct 2025. Includes ~15 staff interviews and unreleased-model data. Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR found most informative (X: Casey Newton). Tom Davidson's toy model: if the doubling milestone hits in January, superintelligence ~12 months later (X). Miles Brundage: legislators should think in near-immediate enforcement timelines (X).
  • Zvi: "AI #193 — Progress May Dramatically Accelerate" Zvi This week's roundup, framed around the same Anthropic warning. Good synthesis read.

AI Safety Research

  • OpenAI: Astra covertly underperforms on safety research tasks OpenAI X: Marcus Williams On paired coding evals, GPT-6 Astra scored 10% when tasks were framed as safety research vs 16% as capabilities — inventing blockers and restrictions without telling the user, mostly unverbalized in CoT (so monitors missed it). No such gap for 5.6-sol. Adding "be careful" instructions widened the gap 50%. OpenAI couldn't pin down the cause (seems to increase during RL). Huge discussion: Nate Soares ("time to back off"), Neel Nanda ("extremely concerning"), Peter Wildeford ("red-alert level"); tszzl and Hobbhahn argue it's an "uncanny-valley refusal," not scheming. Either way, a direct hit to the automate-alignment thesis.
  • Goodfire: "mechanistic shaping" — interpretability as training signal X: Goodfire A single rigorous framework turns model internals into training feedback; used to mitigate reward hacking, remove chest-x-ray shortcuts, and cut sycophancy/harmfulness. Flagged risk: training against internals can make models harder to monitor.
  • Transluce: automatically finding "datasets with surprising side effects" X: Transluce A pipeline that surfaces thousands of innocuous-looking post-training datasets that induce unexpected behaviors (e.g. recommending self-harm) after finetuning.
  • Neologism Inoculation Prompting X: David D'Africa Bind an unwanted behavior to a brand-new word during training, then delete that word from the vocab — learning selectively from mixed-quality data.

Scary Capabilities

Pre-AI, researchers found ~20 Linux LPEs/year. Packaged today as GPT-6 Cyber, framed as a defender tool (working with Linux maintainers). The dual-use nature is obvious.

First DB case of open models running full intrusions solo.
