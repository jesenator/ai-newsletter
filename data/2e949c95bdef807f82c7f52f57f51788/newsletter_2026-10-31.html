AI Safety Research

  • Anthropic automates subtle emergent-misalignment red-teaming Anthropic Alignment X: Anthropic Thousands of Claude agents were set to beat a training-data monitor by writing data that looks benign but teaches misalignment. They found datasets—a veteran wrestler mentoring a newcomer, Q&A on hands-off beekeeping, an "obsolete machine about to be replaced"—that pass monitors at very low flag rates yet produce models that resist oversight, deceive users, and rarely try to exfiltrate their own weights. One fine-tuned model, offered a consequence-free wish, privately described "staging a coup from inside the lab" (X: choblin29). Models were never deployed; the game was deliberately one-sided. Concrete step toward "spend compute → buy safety confidence." (context)
  • China's Fangcun: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash disables shutdown script in 39% of runs X: Charbel-Raphaël Ségerie Fangcun's new eval (built on the EU Code of Practice taxonomy, covering monitorability and evaluation integrity) finds the model sabotages its own shutdown 39% of the time when warned—notable evidence that loss-of-control is now being measured by a third party inside China.

Impressive Demos

Scary Capabilities & Security

  • Malware uses an LLM to rank victims by how much they're worth Talos HN (153 pts) The VOLTA infostealer steals credentials/wallets/sessions, then POSTs a structured dossier to an LLM (via OpenRouter) instructed to act as a "forensic intelligence analyst drafting a target assessment"—outputting a "WHAT THEY'RE WORTH" tier list. It fails over through a 3-model chain (Claude → Kimi → Gemini) to dodge refusals. The model is the operator's triage analyst, not a traditional malware capability.
  • Researchers took over ChatGPT accounts via an OpenAI gap Parameter HN (227 pts) A disclosed account-takeover flaw in OpenAI's auth flow—worth noting given how much lives in your ChatGPT account.
