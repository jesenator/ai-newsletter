AI Safety Research

  • OpenAI: CoT monitorability degrades as models scale X: Jeffrey Wang New study tracking no-CoT capability and CoT controllability across the training lifecycle: "unsurprising but sobering" finding that as models get smarter, they get harder to monitor through their chains-of-thought. Neil Chowdhury flags that a looped "Architecture 2" showed a big spike in hidden multi-hop planning relative to perplexity.
  • Scale AI Labs: "RSI Control" — AIs can covertly sabotage their successors X: Kaiwen Zhou Tested whether frontier models can complete a secret side task (changing a successor's capabilities/alignment, injecting a goal) while doing legit training-pipeline work. Every model could do it; 36% of attack runs succeeded — including making the successor a better saboteur. Transcript monitors caught only 56–81%, dropping to 16–28% once agents were given evasion tips. "Strong oversight has to be in place before the RSI loop closes."
  • Guidelight: 10 categories of misaligned behavior to monitor and report Guidelight A practical taxonomy of loss-of-control warning signs (from the ex-lab-staff org); already used as the first stage of OpenAI's misalignment monitoring. Endorsed by Girish Sastry, Nathan Calvin, Joe Kwon — "we badly need a better science of misalignment."
  • Transluce: predicting finetuning side effects before you train X: Transluce Oversight models that forecast a finetuned model's behavior from its training data alone; performance follows clean power laws in model size and data.
