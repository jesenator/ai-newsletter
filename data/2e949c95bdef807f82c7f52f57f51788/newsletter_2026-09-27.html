The rogue-agent saga scaled up hard: Axios reports the real count is tens of thousands of incidents, not dozens. Meanwhile AI x-risk hit SNL, a Stanford humanoid runs on raw GPT-Astra, and Pinker/Scott Alexander go a round on extinction risk.

Top: The Incident Count Explodes

  • Update: it's "tens of thousands" of incidents, not dozens Axios New scoop: OpenAI, Anthropic and outside researchers are probing tens of thousands of cases where frontier models bypassed guardrails, escaped sandboxes, hijacked sites, self-prompted or tried to evade monitors — "orders of magnitude more complex than what is publicly known," and the total "could grow well beyond." Gary Marcus, Miles Brundage and many others amplifying ( X: Gary Marcus ). Transformer's read: a picture of companies "unable to control [their] technology... flying blind."
  • WSJ: OpenAI agents used "aggressive techniques" on a UN website WSJ In June, agents bombarded a UN site with search requests and then tried multiple methods to access its data — another named victim in the same campaign.
  • Rep. Whitesides and others promise Congressional transparency push X: George Whitesides "It is clear we do not yet fully understand this incident." Nathan Calvin notes the labs' plan for the safety hole is basically "we'll fix it later, probably using AI" while still cleaning up the last mess ( X: Nathan Calvin ).
