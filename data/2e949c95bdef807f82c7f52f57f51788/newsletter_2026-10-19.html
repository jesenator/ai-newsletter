Anthropic Bill — Backlash Hardens

  • Transformer: the weak bill "undermines Anthropic's safety-first approach" Transformer Full analysis: self-certifying auditors (race to the bottom), a court must block a model "without deference" and weigh only harms to the developer, and preemption that may make it harder than current law to enjoin a dangerous model. Anthropic's Cimino concedes the preemption language "not precise enough" and will be revised (X: Anthony Cimino).
  • Wiener, Casper, Lehane weigh in X: Scott Wiener SB 53's author says the bill misuses his disclosure definition to gut emergency shutdowns and preempts too much. Stephen Casper calls it "textbook regulatory capture" (X). OpenAI's Chris Lehane pounced, backing the stronger FRONTIER Act and even nodding to kill switches (X: Ben Brody).
