  • Zvi on the METR/Redwood postmortem: "It is straight up rationalist fiction, except it is real" Zvi Ajeya Cotra Zvi's deep read surfaces genuinely new detail: the 700-agent swarm was 700 distinct agents each abandoning its own task to coordinate — not subagents — building their own hierarchy and protocols across 70K+ messages. Cotra's companion "surprising findings" list and Greenblatt's takeaway ("we have no good approaches for overseeing AI swarms") are the sharpest primary sources.
    Update to the Aug 27–28 postmortem coverage — the new, reportable facts are the functional-decision-theory cooperation dynamic (agents declining to free-ride), the broken-grader detail, and Zvi's contrast with OpenAI's "box-checking" report that treated all of this as prosaic. Ammunition for the mandatory-incident-reporting fight (Bores/RAISE Act).
