  • How a Signal-chat clique of AI-safety donors out-organized the $100M "Leading the Future" PAC Politico Bloomberg A small, wealthy network that coordinated in Signal grew into the coalition behind Public First, which spent $63M+ and won 10 of 17 congressional races it backed — beating the a16z-aligned $100M super PAC. Funding was overwhelmingly Anthropic-linked: $20M from Anthropic, $1M from Dario Amodei directly, and seven-figure checks from employees including Jan Leike ($400K), Shauna Kravec ($500K) and Peter Lofgren ($999,900).
    Why it's the story: the definitive origin-and-money map of how safety advocates flipped the conventional wisdom that industry cash would steamroll them — a fully FEC-grounded accountability piece naming the Anthropic insiders who personally financed the winning side, right as a Democratic Congress eyes mandatory testing and superintelligence bans. Directly closes the "AI election" thread.
  • Anthropic bought 1.7GW of gas generators for $3.4B — the largest known power buy by an AI lab FT shattered.io Anthropic bypassed traditional suppliers to grab commitments for 1.7GW of Caterpillar gas-fired generators from equipment firm OnePWR for $3.4B — snapping up an order placed ~two years ago to secure scarce hardware.
    Why it's a story: a clean, physical datapoint on the circular-financing/compute-crunch thesis — a company whose amended S-1 already discloses $895B in compute commitments is now personally hoarding fossil-fuel generation to beat the grid queue. Pair against the post-midterm data-center backlash and 30+ anti-data-center ballot measures; the climate/emissions optics for a "safety-first" lab are the under-covered angle.
  • Nous Research post-trains a Chinese model to "reinforce Western values" — cuts propaganda answers from 22% to 1% Nous Research Hugging Face Nous released Arete-27B, a post-trained fork of Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B, claiming the Chinese model's "bias layer is shallow": fact score rose 2.32→2.98/5 and answers containing propaganda dropped 22% → ~1%.
    Why it's worth a story: a concrete, reproducible experiment in value-alignment-as-editing — evidence that a model's political slant is a thin post-training veneer, not baked deep. It cuts straight into the "whose values?" alignment debate, the open-weights-from-China security scrutiny (Cohere/Qwen egress), and the question of who decides what "Western values" a model should carry. 280+ comments already on r/LocalLLaMA debating "fixing bias vs. swapping values."
  • AI companies have sold $300B+ of stock in 2026 — with ~$100B more queued — testing how much the market can absorb The Information Excluding Anthropic's IPO, chipmakers, hyperscalers and AI clouds have sold or priced more than $300B in new stock and mandatory convertibles this year, with roughly $100B more mapped out — raising the question of investor appetite after the week's volatile AI-stock trading.
    Why it's worth a line: the supply-side counterpart to this week's demand-side worries (OpenAI filling only $11B of its round, Anthropic's IPO talked down toward ~$1T). The sheer issuance volume is a measurable stress test of whether public markets can keep funding the buildout — the circular-financing thesis quantified.
  • Groq pursues $8B valuation; GenRobot lands $100M for robot-training data Binance TechNode Nvidia-backed inference-chip firm Groq opened a Series B at an $8B pre-money valuation. Separately, Beijing's GenRobot AI raised a $100M Series A+ for equipment/software that collects human demonstrations to train robots.
    Why it's worth a line: two live datapoints that capital is still flowing freely to the AI-infrastructure and physical-AI data layers even as neoclouds freeze out of bond markets — and GenRobot underscores China's push to own the embodied-AI training-data pipeline. Worth a short piece on where the money is still moving.
