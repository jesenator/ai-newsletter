  • Anthropic's threat report accuses every top Chinese lab of fraudulently distilling Claude — Moonshot allegedly passed the outputs off as Kimi's own Zvi Anthropic report Anthropic's Dec 2025–Aug 2026 misuse report spans seven harm areas, but Zvi argues the buried bombshell is distillation: DeepSeek, Moonshot and Xiaomi allegedly funneled bulk queries directly to Claude, with Moonshot then serving results back "as if these were Kimi outputs." Zhipu tried to distill the safety-hardened Fable model, failed, and switched to Opus. Lands two days after a joint NSA/CISA/FBI advisory on Chinese "industrial-scale" distillation.
    Why it's the story: this is a concrete, attributable escalation of the distillation fight that anchors Amodei's "pace the frontier" plan and the US–China talks this month — and it directly antagonizes Beijing days before Xi's expected DC visit. Reportable: whether the query-laundering claim is technically substantiated, and how Moonshot/DeepSeek respond. The rest of the report (Russian Midnight Blizzard automating malware evasion, a Chinese-speaking op compromising ~50 orgs) is a clean "sophisticated attacks no longer need sophisticated attackers" warning-shot piece.

Same day, Musk called for top US labs and "three or four leading Chinese companies" to let rivals run a "test harness" on each other's models.
    Why it's the story: directly advances the antitrust-as-barrier-to-safety thread from Sept 11 (OpenAI had asked Congress whether an industry slowdown would breach antitrust). OpenAI is now publicly declaring the legal question moot — a checkable claim worth testing against DOJ/FTC posture. Musk's cross-lab test-harness pitch is the first concrete inter-lab evaluation mechanism floated by a rival principal; press whether any of this is real or PR ahead of the Cruz bill.
