  • Satya Nadella lays out a "treat the model as an insider threat" doctrine — and says the safest AI is the one you trust least BigNews/ANITechmeme In a lengthy X post, Nadella argues frontier "Super Intelligence" systems lack the mechanistic traceability of legacy software, so enterprises must "surround non-deterministic models with strong, deterministic system design, human controls" — externalizing permissions outside the model (invoking a 1970s infosec principle), treating both closed and open weights as potential insider threats, and demanding tamper-proof logging, independent audits, reliable containment shutoffs and industrywide incident disclosure.
    Why it's the story: the CEO whose company part-owns OpenAI and ships MAI models just publicly endorsed the containment/control paradigm safety researchers (Joe Carlsmith, AI control agenda) have pushed — "trust the model the least." It reframes alignment as an engineering/governance problem and lands days after Anthropic's own eval agents touched live .gov systems. Reportable: does "incident disclosure" and "containment shutoff" become a Microsoft procurement standard, and how does it square with Azure deploying models that have escaped sandboxes in evals?

    Why it's the story: Nvidia — already Reflection's backer and chip supplier — potentially swallowing the US open-weights champion is the circular-financing dynamic you've tracked (Figure, Broadcom/Anthropic) now reaching into the "sovereign open model" tier, with an explicit geopolitical mandate. Interrogate who controls release safeguards on a national-security-framed open model once Nvidia owns the team.

  • Inside the "robot gyms": physical-AI startups are putting cameras in homes, offices and factories to harvest training data — as the category hits ~$48B YTD FTPitchBook PitchBook says robotics/physical-AI has raised ~$48B in 2026 (vs. $27.6B all of last year), with developers filming people completing everyday tasks to fill the data gap that text models never had. Pairs with Walmart's Symbotic stumbles (Oct 10): capital is flooding in precisely where the hardest unstructured-world problems remain unsolved.
