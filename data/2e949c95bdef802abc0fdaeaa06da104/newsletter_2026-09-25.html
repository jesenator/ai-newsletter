    Why it matters: A rare quantitative, primary-source read on whether swarms unlock speed or genuine capability — and a concrete number to plug into RSI/loss-of-control debates. Strong explainer peg connecting the Hugging Face 1,200-agent coordination incident to inference-scaling economics.
