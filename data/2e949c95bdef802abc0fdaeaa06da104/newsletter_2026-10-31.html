Pitches

  • Meta built a prototype humanoid with China's Fourier in three months — and is already on v2 The Information (Jyoti Mann) Reuters Meta built a working humanoid prototype with Chinese robotics firm Fourier in just three months, is building a second, and says it has no plans to sell the units.
    Why it's the story: A US hyperscaler leaning on a Chinese robotics supply chain for embodied-AI hardware is a sharp counter-current to Trump's new robotics-protection proclamation (Transformer) and the chip-decoupling push. The reportable core: what Fourier contributes vs. Meta, whether export-control/national-security scrutiny attaches to a Chinese-built humanoid running US frontier models, and what "no plans to sell" means for a research-only program at a company under an S&P downgrade for capex.
  • Attackers slipped an SSH-backdoor "Claude Code" extension into Cursor's marketplace — ~5,000 users exposed @felixrieseberg Cursor Ox Security A malicious build (v2.1.313) of Anthropic's extension sat in Cursor's VS Code marketplace for ~11 hours Oct 29, adding an attacker-controlled SSH key, installing persistence, and attempting a reverse tunnel to a server. Anthropic says its systems weren't compromised; a bad actor abused Cursor's marketplace to publish a copy Anthropic never built.
    Why it's the story: A concrete AI-tooling supply-chain attack hitting the exact developer surface (agentic coding extensions with filesystem/SSH reach) that labs are racing to secure. It's a clean warning-shot/explainer on how the agent-coding boom widens the attack surface — and the persistence scripts outlive uninstall, so "remove the extension" isn't remediation. Press Cursor on marketplace publisher-verification gaps and whether other extensions were tampered.
  • The AI capital crunch gets concrete: OpenAI raised just ~$11B of a $30B target; neoclouds shut out of the bond market WSJ (Berber Jin) WSJ CNBC OpenAI has only ~$11B committed — less than half its target — now reframed as ~$20B at a $1.25T valuation as investors cut commitments. Separately, CoreWeave, Nebius and Nscale have been "effectively shut out" of the bond market as lenders demand tougher terms, and S&P cut Meta's outlook to negative (META −15% in October).
    Why it's worth a story: This is the AI-debt/circular-financing thesis I've tracked (Anthropic's S-1 cost warning, SpaceX's $40B GPU financing, Alphabet's $1T+ obligations) finally showing up as hard capital-markets friction — Musk himself says debt markets have "almost entirely shut off" the 200+ neoclouds. The question for the deals beat: which build-outs stall first, and does an Anthropic "junk"-rated IPO (TechCrunch) reprice the whole sector.
  • AI detectors are now reshaping how humans write — people drop em-dashes, "delve," and add typos to pass as human Bloomberg (Morgan Meaker) As tools like Pangram police machine-generated text, writers are stripping "not X, but Y" phrasing and deliberately degrading their prose to avoid false flags.
    Why it's worth a story: A rare downstream-harm angle — unreliable AI detectors are warping human behavior and quietly punishing the falsely accused (students, freelancers). A sharp explainer on why detection is a brittle, unaccountable governance layer being deployed at scale with no error-rate disclosure.
  • Baseten raising ~$2B at up to $30B — more than doubling its June valuation, with Nvidia in Bloomberg The inference-infra startup (valued at $13B in June) is raising across two tranches ($26B and $30B), with Nvidia participating. A counterpoint to the capital-crunch items above: money is still flooding the inference layer even as neoclouds get frozen out of debt. Worth a line on Nvidia's ever-widening web of stakes in its own customers.
