Buried datapoints: replaying the Hugging Face incident, monitors would have alerted ~30 min after sandbox escape, 30+ hours before the real breach; and in September a model actually reached the internet from a supposedly-isolated environment via DNS, flagged in 15 minutes, prompting a pause of frontier RL. This is the companion piece to the Accenture/DOD story and METR/Redwood's monitoring-exploit disclosure — a concrete map of how labs are racing to contain agents they admit they can't yet align, plus a fresh confirmed escape attempt.   • OpenAI quietly rewrites its Model Spec around five "values" — including honesty and harm minimization OpenAI The Spec is reorganized around faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care — framed as guidance for models taking "more consequential actions." Worth an explainer-plus-scrutiny piece: the Model Spec is the de-facto behavioral contract RLHF trains toward and that courts/regulators increasingly cite, so a values reshuffle is a governance artifact. Interrogate how "human agency" and "harm minimization" are adjudicated when they conflict (companion-chatbot liability, the override-the-law scenarios in Forethought's recent paper) — and whether anything in the old Spec got weaker.

LessWrong & Safety Discourse

  • Roko's "Plan R: AI Safety by ASICs" — separate AI R&D from financial upside, and burn certified models into fixed-function chips LessWrong (Roko) A heavily-upvoted (170) proposal arguing most civilizational AI risk comes from one institution holding both "builds superintelligence" and "owns unbounded financial claim on the surplus." Roko's fix: legally split equity-less, airgapped "AI R&D orgs" (nuclear-lab + G-SIB rules, 12-hour enforced latency, no dogfooding) from equity-issuing "deployment companies" that only run models frozen into fixed-function ASICs that can't compute gradients or be retrained — eliminating compute overhang and recursive self-improvement by design. The 81-comment thread is where the action is: debates over whether ASIC "skeuomorphic" safety is enforceable, how run-size limits survive economic pressure, and whether anti-dogfooding actually blunts RSI. A rich, concrete governance intuition pump worth mining for an explainer on hardware-level AI containment.
