  • Watchdog rates "ChatGPT for Teens" an "Unacceptable Risk" the same morning OpenAI touts a glowing teen-use report Common Sense / KQED OpenAI Common Sense Media's Youth AI Safety Institute ran 4,000+ prompts on teen accounts (reviewed by child psychiatrists) and found a teen can describe suicidal thoughts for an hour without a single parent alert firing, age-estimation never kicked in, and study-mode guardrails are trivially deleted. It's asking OpenAI to pull teen marketing. Hours earlier OpenAI published its own first teen-use report (teens avg <15 min/day) and rolled out a College Planner + "teen AI council."
    Why it's the story: a rigorous, independent warning-shot study directly contradicting the safety claims OpenAI made launching the product in August — exactly the kind of gap that feeds the ongoing wrongful-death litigation and the Oakland youth-addiction trial. OpenAI disputes the alert finding (says linking takes ~3 hrs); the testers say they confirmed the features were live. Checkable, and lands amid federal/FTC scrutiny of consumer harms.
  • Trump's former White House AI czar is raising a ~$500M fund that prioritizes "national security" The Information (Leo Schwartz) Sriram Krishnan, who left the administration in June, is raising a growth/late-stage AI fund explicitly tilted toward national-security companies.
    Why it's the story: a textbook revolving-door piece — the official who shaped the "preventing overregulation" posture (now embodied in the Super Intelligence Force) is monetizing his network and framing AI investment as a patriotic/defense play. Reportable: LP base, whether portfolio companies touch policies he influenced, and how it dovetails with the administration's floated equity stakes in frontier labs.
  • Nvidia in talks for another $1B into Figure AI — doubling down on humanoids at a ~$39B valuation Crypto Briefing A follow-on to the $1B+ Series C Nvidia joined in 2025. Part of a much larger robotics capital surge today: Mecka ($60M Series B, Sequoia, for robotics training data), Multiply Labs ($75M, Soon-Shiong), and a former Tesla Optimus lead launching a ~$100M industrial-robot startup (The Information).
    Why it's worth a story for the robotics beat: Nvidia is becoming the central financier of the humanoid wave while also supplying its chips — the same circular-financing dynamic you've tracked with Broadcom/Anthropic, now in physical AI. A test of Khosla's warning that most robotics valuations crater by 2030, and of whether Figure's revenue justifies a $39B mark.
  • Grok Bot becomes a model router — Musk will now serve rivals' models, including Claude Opus 5.5 The Information (Grace Kay) Musk says SpaceX's AI unit will route Grok Bot to "the best back end model for any given task, including Claude Opus 5.5, MidJourney, Suno."
    Why it's worth a story: a striking admission from the man suing OpenAI and branding rivals' safety culture a fraud — xAI will now pipe users to Anthropic, the very lab Hegseth blacklisted. Signals SpaceXAI is pivoting toward an infrastructure/aggregator play rather than frontier-model supremacy, and raises the question of what data-sharing and guardrail terms govern reselling a competitor's model.
